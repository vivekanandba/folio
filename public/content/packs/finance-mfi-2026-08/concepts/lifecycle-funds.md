# Life-cycle funds — the glide path arrives in India

> Pick the year you'll need the money. The fund handles everything else — including getting more careful as the date approaches.

> [!key] India's first **life-cycle (target-date) funds** are here — Zerodha launched (passively run, Nifty LargeMidcap 250 sleeve), ICICI Prudential filed (actively run). Each fund is built around **one target year in its name** (2031/2036/2041/2046/2051); the **glide path** shifts the mix from equity toward debt automatically as the year nears. In the US the category holds ~**$4.8 trillion** as the workplace default; India's NPS has had a glide path for years, but voluntary adoption stayed thin.

## SEBI's rulebook

- **Glide-path bands** the manager must stay within (bands are ranges — two funds with the same year can differ).
- Non-core holdings (gold/silver ETFs, InvITs, ETCDs) capped at **10% combined**; equity and debt drive returns. Return-flattering words barred from names.
- At most **six schemes** per fund house; near maturity, debt restricted to **AA-rated and above** maturing before the target date; within a year of maturity a fund may merge into the nearest-maturity scheme with unitholder consent.
- **Graded exit loads** instead of a lock-in: 3% within one year, 2% within two, 1% within three. Benchmarked like multi-asset; base TER capped at **2.1%**.

> [!tip] **Life-cycle fund vs NPS**: the NPS glide (Auto Choice) is retirement-shaped — Tier I locks money for 15 years or to age 60, and rewards patience with up to 60% tax-free withdrawal. A life-cycle fund is an **open wrapper you can point at any dated goal**, redeemable after the exit-load window. They overlap but do different jobs — and can sit side by side.

> [!more] The passive-first curiosity
> A brand-new category with no Indian track record launched **passive first** — a sign of how far index investing has traveled here. What the bands leave unsettled: how much an active manager can add inside SEBI's ranges. That answer arrives only with time.

## Investor move

- Match the **fund's year to the goal's year** — that's the entire selection.
- Used as intended it's a **single-fund plan**: allocation, rebalancing, de-risking included.
- Check the **glide within the bands** — same target year ≠ same path.
