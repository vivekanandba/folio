# SIFs — rich people's funds, democratized (and barely used)

> One year in, the headline freedom SIFs are sold on — going short — is not actually being used.

> [!key] **Specialized Investment Funds** sit between mutual funds and PMS/AIFs: the **same SEBI oversight as mutual funds**, but with PMS/AIF-style freedoms — **short securities they don't own**, concentrate in a few sectors, move freely across asset classes, set their own redemption windows — at a **₹10 lakh** minimum instead of PMS's ₹50 lakh or an AIF's ₹1 crore. The August 2026 cover story's finding: most managers have **barely used the shorting freedom**, so early performance can't yet be read as a verdict on it.

## The long-short promise

Beneath most SIFs is one strategy: a **long book** of holdings expected to rise and a **short book** through derivatives to profit from expected fallers — two opposing bets meant to smooth the ride: *capture upside, cushion downside*.

> [!warn] The audit says otherwise. In a monthly hit-rate grid against the Nifty 500, most SIFs and the index barely diverged — **except March–April 2026**, when the market broke. Only one equity long-short fund delivered "protect first, chase the rally second"; peers "barely cushioned at all," and one rode the rebound to finish ahead *without* protecting when it mattered. Shorting takes conviction and the willingness to be **visibly wrong** — a hard position to take boldly and repeatedly.

> [!more] How to read a fledgling category
> The earliest funds aren't a year old. The honest conclusions: (1) you're paying for freedoms that may sit unused; (2) judge a long-short fund on the months that *test* the short book, not the average month; (3) the category's value will only show across a full cycle. The same label-vs-behavior discipline as [flexi-cap drift](#/pack/finance-mfi-2026-07/concept/style-drift-flexi-tax): read holdings and behavior, not the pitch.

## Investor move

- Know the ladder: **MF → SIF (₹10L) → PMS (₹50L) → AIF (₹1cr)** — same regulator gaze only on the first two.
- Judge long-short funds on **crash months**, not calm ones.
- Paying for freedom? **Verify it's being used.**
