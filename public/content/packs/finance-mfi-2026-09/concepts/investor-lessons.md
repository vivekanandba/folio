> The September special hands the microphone to readers — and their stories converge on the same few disciplines.

## Two portraits worth keeping

- The **career break**: stepping away from a paycheck without the plan stepping away too — the corpus and SIPs sized *before* the break, so independence survived the income gap.
- The **74-year-old fan** whose father raised eight people, funded five weddings and still gave to hospitals on a government salary — saving as a family technology, upgraded by the next generation into investing ("My father always saved. I started investing").

> [!more] Goals with names
> The FD "for her wedding" that became investments **for the daughter's dreams** — renaming the goal changed the product, the horizon, and who the money serves. Goals inherit assumptions; audit the assumption before the allocation. (The [four pillars](#/pack/finance-mfi-2026-07/concept/four-pillars-planning) formalize what these stories learned the hard way.)

## Investor move

- Pre-commit to the SIP **before** the fall makes the decision for you.
- Keep an **emergency floor** outside the long-term corpus.
- **Step up contributions with income**; let the raise compound.
