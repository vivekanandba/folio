# Reading 2026 — flat years, slumps, and who really sold IT

> Seven months of 2026, and the average flexi-cap NAV is back where it started. That's not nothing — that's data.

> [!key] The year so far: flexi-cap **+0.6%**, large-cap **−2.6%**, ELSS **−0.9%** — a flat stretch that tests plans, not funds. Small caps are the real bruise: **−13.2%** this year, **−7.8%** over one, and a three-year return of **5.5% a year — less than a liquid fund** over the same period. And Indian IT has fallen for a year and a half: **Nifty IT −41.5%** from December 2024 to July 2026, nearly two-thirds of it in 2026 alone.

## The IT anatomy — a de-rating, not an earnings crash

- The market cut what it pays for a rupee of IT profit from **~28× earnings (Jan 2026) to under 17× (July)** — *while earnings rose*. The fall priced tomorrow's growth, not today's profits.
- The AI worry has teeth: billable-hours revenue shrinks if clients do the same work with fewer people. June-quarter revenue at the big five rose ~14% in rupees — but strip the rupee's fall and **real growth was low single digits** (Infosys 2.4%, HCL 2.6%, TCS 3.2%). Not what a 28× valuation was built on.

## Two counterpoints from the issue

- **PPFAS deploys**: Parag Parikh Flexi Cap's cash has fallen from ~25% toward 14–15%, headed for single digits — a famously patient allocator finding prices worth paying. Monthly disclosures will verify.
- **The AI-bubble frame** (Chollet): *"A bubble is not a property of the technology. It is a property of the people buying"* — a thing can be a bubble **even if the tech works and demand is real**. Prices, not products, make bubbles.

> [!more] What a flat year is for
> Nothing happened to the NAV; plenty happened to the investor. Flat stretches are when SIPs accumulate quietly, when small-cap three-year numbers finally tell the truth the rally hid, and when the discipline bought in [August's issue](#/pack/finance-mfi-2026-08/concept/market-discipline) pays its rent.

## Investor move

- Judge slumps by **what got re-priced** (growth expectations) vs what broke (earnings).
- Know whether your funds track the **median or the AUM-weighted** behavior.
- Let flat years do their job: **accumulate, rebalance, verify claims against disclosures**.
